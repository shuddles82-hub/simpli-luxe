LIFESTYLE & HOME

Sunset Muse (Fall Edition)

A cozy yet refreshing mix of citrus and spice; the warmth of a Moscow Mule, the brightness of blood orange, and the comfort of classic lemonade. Think autumn sunset in a glass.

Ingredients

3 oz Moscow Mule mocktail (zero sugar), 2 oz blood orange Italian sparkling beverage, 2 oz lemonade, fresh strawberry slices. Optional: red sugar or cinnamon-sugar rim.

How To Make It

Rim your glass with sugar. Add ice and pour in the mocktail, lemonade, and blood orange soda. Stir gently and garnish with sliced strawberries or a dehydrated orange wheel.
